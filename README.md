# Bookmarks - A Simple Social Website

Bookmarks is a lightweight social platform where you can share blog posts and your favorite links. It’s designed to be simple, clean, and easy to use—ideal for users who want to browse, read, and share interesting content.

---

## Overview

Bookmarks is a basic social website that mimics the look and feel of a blog. It allows users to:
- Read and share blog posts.
- Save and manage their favorite bookmarks.
- Enjoy a responsive, user-friendly interface on both desktop and mobile devices.

---

## Features

- **Simple Blog Interface:** A clean and minimalist design for reading and writing posts.
- **Bookmark Sharing:** Easily share links and articles with the community.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for various devices.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Focused on simplicity and ease of use.
